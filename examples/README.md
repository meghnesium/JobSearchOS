# Examples

Everything here is invented to show the shape of finished files. The persona, Alex Rivera, and the employer, Northwind Analytics, do not exist and every metric is made up. The three companies are real so you can see how a dossier references a real product, but the statements about them are deliberately generic and marked `[verify]`. Do your own research. Never copy a number from an example into your own letter.

| Folder | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `profile-example/` | A filled-in pillar and a filled-in routing table, so you can see what "defensible numbers with a method and a caveat" looks like |
| `jobs/3-stripe.md` and `3-stripe-letter.html` | Status `drafted`: research and letter done, not sent yet (prefix `3-`) |
| `jobs/2-miro.md`, `2-miro-letter.html` and `2-miro-resume.html` | Status `applied`: sent, follow-up date set, tailored resume made (prefix `2-`) |
| `jobs/1-jetbrains.md` and `1-jetbrains-letter.html` | Status `screening`: interview prep sections auto-added once (prefix `1-`) |

Notice the filenames. Same company, three moments, three prefixes, no dates in the names. Moving a job forward is just `/log-application Stripe applied`, and the files rename themselves.

## Open the HTML

Each `-letter.html` is a finished structured letter built from the shipped template with an invented teal-and-serif brand. Open it in a browser to see the layout and press **Export PDF** to save it. The files are generated by `scripts/build-examples.py`, so they stay in step with the template.
