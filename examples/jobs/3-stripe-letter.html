Hello Stripe team,

I'm looking for a team where design owns the system as well as the screens. Stripe's dashboard serves a finance lead and a support agent from the same components, which is the kind of shared structure I've spent three years building at Northwind Analytics, where I work on executive reporting today. My habit is to decide once, write it down and let others reuse it, and I would like to do that at your scale.

That habit started with one stubborn problem, so I picked a similar one on your side. It comes from reading how a merchant works through a disputed payment, which I enjoyed more than I expected.

One challenge I worked through

A merchant opens a disputed payment and needs to decide whether to fight it or accept it. To answer, they move through the payment view, the customer view, the evidence form and the deadline list. Each page is correct on its own, and the decision lives across all four.

Source: public help-centre page on responding to disputes [verify]

This example is from my current reporting work at Northwind, because that is where I met the same shape of problem.

At Stripe

  • Four views for one decision
  • Evidence and deadline live on separate pages

At Northwind Analytics

  • One panel per decision in every Northwind report
  • Evidence and deadline shown side by side
What I did about it
→

At Northwind that panel stopped being one report's fix. It became the rule for every report after it, with no second executive version to maintain, and the next report took 3 working sessions instead of 6, by my own log over four reports (small sample, same team).

From the outside I can only see the surface, so this is a guess about the inside.

You know your constraints better than I do: regulation, older views, a dashboard that many teams touch at once. Within those limits, I would enjoy a surface where one decision crosses four pages and the fix has to hold for a finance lead and a support agent alike. If it helps, I can walk you through how the Northwind panel was argued for and cut.

Happy to walk you through it.
