How to cut the bill
  • Run setup and search synthesis on the small model. They are questions, templates and summarising.
  • Write letters on the mid model. The large model rarely produces a better letter and costs several times more.
  • Skip phases you do not need. Warm intros and interview prep are optional modules.
  • Use the local script for the Friday filename check. It costs nothing.
  • Set the sweep to fewer nights. One sweep a night is the biggest line item.
  • Kill weak applications at the Decision Card. A killed application costs one turn, not two.

Estimates are rough: a fast letter is one consolidated search plus two turns, a sweep run covers about 40 searches and 3 to 4 drafts. Real numbers vary with the size of your profile files and the pages searched.
