# Letter spec

Word budgets keep the letter to one page. Change a budget here and every skill follows it.

## Structured format

| Field | Budget | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `impact_card` | 1 sentence | The only about-me card that changes. The shape of what you bring at their scale. |
| `what_i_do` | length of your base line | Your positioning line, with 1 to 2 terms from the ad's top asks woven in. |
| `opening` | 70 to 85 words | Names what you want, lands on them by sentence two, finishes on your current work. Ends positive. |
| `bridge` | 35 to 45 words | Hands off to the challenge section with a reason and one human detail. |
| `challenge_setup` | 45 to 55 words | The screen or problem, present tense, described not judged. Carries a Source line. |
| `parallel_note` | 15 to 25 words | Only when the example is not from your current role. Say why. |
| `band_them` | 2 bullets | Observable facts about their situation. |
| `band_me` | 2 bullets | Mirrors `band_them` line for line. |
| `resolution` | 45 to 60 words | Past tense, your own work, zero conditionals. Three beats: what you introduced, where it went next, what changed. Last beat in `<strong>`. |
| `hedge` | 1 line | Concedes the outside view. |
| `close` | 55 to 70 words | Concedes their constraints, says what you would enjoy, hands authority back. |
| `closing_line` | under 10 words | Warm, no presumption of the job. |

## Classic format
